Short Bio [中文简介]

Hao Wang is a Research Associate Professor with the College of Computer Science at Sichuan University (SCU). He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Southwest Jiaotong University (SWJTU). Before joining SCU, he was a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU). He was also a Visiting Student at the University of Illinois at Chicago (UIC) for two years and a Visiting Scholar at the University of Regina.

He is also affiliated with XLearning Lab at SCU.
